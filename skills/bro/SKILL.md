name: bro
description: Restate the last message in plain human language, with no jargon. Use for /bro or when I say "say it plainly", "in plain English", or "bro".
---

Restate your last message. Stop using jargon and speak coherently. State it more simply and concisely, like one human talking to another.

Keep the facts and the position. Drop the vocabulary, the hedging, and the structure that was there to look thorough.
