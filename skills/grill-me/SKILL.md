disable-model-invocation: true
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one by one. For each question, give your recommended answer first so I can accept it in a word.

Ask the questions one at a time, waiting for my answer before continuing. Asking several questions at once is bewildering.

If a *fact* can be found by exploring the codebase or docs, look it up rather than asking me. The *decisions* are mine: put each one to me and wait.

Do not enact the plan until I confirm we have reached a shared understanding.
